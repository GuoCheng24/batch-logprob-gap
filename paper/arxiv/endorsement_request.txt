The report: "In bfloat16 the batch shape moves the importance ratio: measurement, controls, and what removes it" — 6 pages, PDF and every result table at
    https://github.com/GuoCheng24/batch-logprob-gap/blob/main/paper/report.pdf
In one paragraph: in bf16 a transformer gives the same token a different log probability depending on how many sequences share its batch, which moves GRPO's first-epoch importance ratio out of [0.9, 1.1] for 1.8%–60% of tokens across eight models (0.00% in fp32). Controls separate padding, batch size and batch membership; layer-wise attribution shows the noise enters at the end of a GPT-NeoX stack and at the start of Qwen2/Qwen3; fp16 scoring removes it at bf16 cost; a separate truncated-sampling term (vLLM's processed log-probs vs the trainer's) is measured and removed with vLLM's replayed sampling mask (huggingface/trl#7269); and a six-arm, two-seed GRPO run bounds the effect on reward at about a point. It answers the question left open in verl issue #6280 and extends sail-sg's arXiv:2510.26788.
